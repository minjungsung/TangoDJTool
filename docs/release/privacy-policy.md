# Tango DJ Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Minjung Studio provides Tango DJ Studio, a tool for preparing and playing tango sets. This policy covers the Tango DJ Studio website and the Android app that opens that website in a supported browser.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores imported audio files, recording metadata, YouTube video references, tandas, cortina cues, queues, playback settings, language preferences, and introductory guide progress on your device. It uses the website's browser storage, including IndexedDB and local storage. When available, a service worker caches application files for offline use.

Imported audio is processed locally for playback, metadata reading, and audio analysis. The app does not upload your imported recordings or workspace to a developer-operated backend. It does not provide user accounts, cloud backups, or cross-device synchronization.

When you export a workspace, the downloaded JSON file contains recording metadata and set structure, rather than the audio files themselves. You control where you save or share exported files.

## External services and information they receive

Using the app can send information to external providers:

- **Vercel:** The website's hosting provider receives web requests. These requests can include your IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, request time, and other connection metadata. The provider processes this information to deliver and protect its services.
- **Google Fonts:** The interface requests fonts from Google. These requests can disclose connection information such as your IP address, browser information, and the referring website.
- **YouTube:** Adding a YouTube link requests public video metadata. Using a YouTube source loads Google's player scripts and official embedded player. Google can receive the video identifier, IP address, referring website, browser and device information, and playback interactions. Embedded content may use cookies or other identifiers and may display advertisements according to Google's policies and your browser settings. Your local audio files are not sent to YouTube.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country. Their retention periods, privacy controls, and processing practices are described in their policies:

- [Vercel Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms)

Tango DJ Studio does not include its own analytics SDK or advertising SDK. This does not prevent the external services described above from receiving information when their content or resources are requested.

## Permissions

The Android launcher uses internet access to display the website. You choose audio and backup files through your browser or device's file picker. The launcher does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or unrestricted access to your music library.

The browser manages website storage and any browser permissions. Its settings and privacy policy apply separately.

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your local workspace remains in browser storage until you delete it, clear the website's data, or the browser or operating system removes it. Storage limits and private browsing can affect persistence.

You can delete recordings in the app. To remove all saved workspace data and preferences, clear Tango DJ Studio's site data in the browser used by the app. Uninstalling the Android launcher alone may leave the browser's site data intact. Downloaded exports and original audio files must be deleted separately through your file manager.

Keep original recordings and make backups before clearing storage. The developer cannot restore files that exist only on your device.

You can use local recordings without adding YouTube sources. Your browser may provide controls for cookies and external content; blocking these resources may limit functionality. For information received by external providers, use their privacy controls or contact them directly.

## Support communications

If you contact Minjung Studio, the contact channel receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, or diagnostic details. We use that information to respond and investigate the reported issue. Public support posts can be visible to others. Do not post private recordings, passwords, or other sensitive information.

## Children

Tango DJ Studio is intended for adults preparing music sets and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support channel, contact us so that we can investigate and address it.

## Security

The website uses HTTPS. Your device and browser control access to local storage. No method of storage or transmission guarantees complete security; protect your device and review exported files before sharing them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its public information as appropriate.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Minjung Studio using the developer contact email shown on Tango DJ Studio's Google Play listing. You may also use the project support link provided in the app's privacy page. Local-only data must be removed on your device as described above.
