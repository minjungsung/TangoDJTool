# Google Play listing draft

## App name

Tango DJ Studio

## Short description

Prepare tango tandas, set cortina cues, and play your own recordings.

## Full description

Prepare a tango set on your phone or tablet with Tango DJ Studio.

Add your own recordings, organize them into tandas, and choose start and end cues for cortinas. Use Music, Set, and Play to move from preparation to playback without crowding the phone screen.

- Import local audio and edit title, orchestra, singer, year, style, and BPM.
- Search your library and arrange tandas and cortinas.
- Play a prepared queue, pause, seek, and move between recordings.
- Apply entrance and endpoint fades to supported local audio.
- Analyze local recording levels and adjust playback gain.
- Save your workspace on your device and export set structure as JSON.
- Explore example recordings through official YouTube embeds.
- Use Korean, English, or Spanish interface controls.

Your imported audio stays in the site's browser storage on your device. JSON backups include metadata and set structure; keep your original audio files and relink them when transferring to another device.

YouTube needs an internet connection and embedding permission. Some mobile devices ignore YouTube volume changes. Preview shares the main output; separate headphone monitoring is unavailable. Browser codec support and device storage vary. Rehearse your actual audio setup before live use.

The Android app uses a supported browser to run the Tango DJ Studio website. After a successful online launch, the cached app and saved local recordings can be used offline. Initial installation and YouTube sources require a connection. Background playback is not guaranteed.
